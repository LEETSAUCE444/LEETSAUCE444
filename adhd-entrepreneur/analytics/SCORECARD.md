# Weekly scorecard

**What this is:** Zucc logs the numbers every night in `daily.csv`. Claude runs the weekly review from that file.

**Weeks** run Thursday to Wednesday, because Day 1 was a Thursday:
- Week 1 = Days 1–7, Oct 22–28.
- The review lands in the Thursday pre-post.

`analytics-log.md` stays as Zucc's written notes. `daily.csv` holds the numbers.

## The headline number: saves + shares per view (SSR)

| Platform | SSR = | Where Zucc reads it |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | (saves + shares) ÷ reach | Insights on the post (needs a Professional account, see below) |
| TikTok | (saves + shares) ÷ views | TikTok Studio, per video |
| YouTube Shorts | shares ÷ views (Studio has no "saves" for Shorts) | YouTube Studio |
| X | (bookmarks + reposts) ÷ views | post analytics in the app (Wil's phone) |

- **Read numbers in the apps, not from public pages.** Public pages hide reach, saves and shares on Instagram, and lag on TikTok.
- **Instagram Insights only exist on a Professional account** (Creator or Business). If @adhdentrepreneurhq is a personal account, switching is free, but it's an account change, so it's **Wil's call**.
- **Read each post again the next evening,** about 35 hours after posting. The weekly review uses that read, because same-day numbers are early.
- **Under 100 views, don't compare SSR between posts.** One save swings it wildly. Compare the raw counts instead.

**Also tracked per platform:** followers (start and end of week), profile visits, follows from the post, and comments. Likes are secondary.

## The weekly review

1. **Platform table:** posts, median views, median SSR, best post, comments, followers start → end, profile visits.
2. **By content type:** median SSR for reminders, tips, AI and founder posts, plus reel vs collage.
3. **One sentence** on what the numbers say.
4. **One experiment** for next week.
5. **One thing to stop.**

## Decision rules

These are our own rules, measured against our own numbers, not industry benchmarks.

- **Wait for the data.** No strategy change on a platform before it has 7 posts. Fixing something broken is always allowed.
- **One change at a time.** Run it for 7 posts, then compare the medians with the previous 7.
- **Niche down** when one content type's median SSR is **at least double** the overall median for 2 weeks running, with 4 or more posts of that type. Then shift the next calendar's mix 10 points toward it. Example: 40/30/20/10 becomes 50/20/20/10.
- **Change the post time** only after week 2, and only if a platform's median views fall 2 weeks in a row while the other platforms hold. Test a slot at least 3 hours away for 7 posts.
- **Change the format** if a platform's median SSR is zero or the lowest for 2 weeks (10+ posts). Test one change there:
  - a shorter video (10–15 s)
  - the dark card
  - the platform's own in-app sound instead of the music baked into the video
- **Collage:** after 14 collages on a platform, keep it where its median SSR is at least the reel's median, or where it clearly brings follows. Otherwise drop it there.
- **Problems come first:** a warning, a strike, muted audio, or a post stuck at 0 views. Fix those before reading anything else, and pause any test while you do.
- **Never:** bought followers or engagement, engagement pods, or follow-unfollow.
