# Draft: follow-up email after a missed build goal

**Status:** draft (Oct 27). Wil asked for it on Oct 27. Nothing gets sent until kits are on sale and Wil approves the final wording and the discount size.

**When:** 1 to 3 days after the deadline passes without an accepted proof. Send it once, to that buyer only.

**Rules:**
- The offer appears only here, never on the site, the quiz or the policies.
- No guilt or pressure, and no fake countdown.
- The deadline on the offer is real.
- Nothing about income. The goal stays something built.
- Plain text, from the brand inbox, faceless.

**Open:**
- the discount: 50% or 75% (`q-miss-offer` on the Questions tab)
- what it applies to: the next kit, or a continuation of the same kit
- how long the offer lasts
- whether the continuation carries the money-back guarantee

---

**Subject:** Your {kit name}: here's a way to finish

Hey {first name},

The deadline on your {kit name} passed before the goal was done. That happens. The kit is still yours, and so is everything you built so far.

If you want another run at it, here's an offer that's only for people in your spot:

**{discount}% off {the continuation of your kit / your next kit}**, with a fresh deadline. It picks up where you stopped: {one line on what it covers, e.g. "the follow-up and review steps you hadn't set up yet"}.

Use code **{code}** by **{date}**: {link}

{Only if Wil decides the continuation carries the guarantee:} Same deal as before: finish the goal by the new deadline, send the proof, and you get your money back.

Or just reply and tell us what got in the way. We read every one, and it shapes what we build next.
