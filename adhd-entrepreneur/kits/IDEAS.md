# Starter kit ideas, by niche

This is a running list. **Claude keeps it. Zucc adds research. Wil decides what gets built.** Nothing here is a promise or a price.

Each idea lists:
- who it's for
- the build goal that earns the refund (see `PLAN.md`)
- what's in the box
- the next kit if they miss the goal
- what research we still need

**Status:** `idea` → `researching` → `drafting` → `testing` → `ready for Wil` → `approved`

Every goal is a **draft until Wil approves it**. Goals are always about something built, never money earned.

### K1. The Operator: an AI crew for a business owner
*From the Oct 22 brief: "The Operator Loadout — Claude Crew Starter."*

- **For:** someone already running a business who wants AI handling the daily admin: inbox triage, follow-ups, scheduling notes, research, first drafts.
- **Build goal (draft):** their AI crew handles 3 real daily tasks for 7 days in a row, with a short log as proof.
- **In the box:**
  - crew instructions for Claude (projects and roles)
  - the same set adapted for ChatGPT and Gemini
  - the daily routine
  - the setup guide
- **Next kit if they miss:** K7 (local service) or K4 (all-in-one), depending on the blocker.
- **Research needed:** which Claude plan features the kit relies on, current as of launch.
- **Status:** idea

### K2. The daily content system: the setup this account runs on
- **For:** someone who wants to post every day and keeps stopping.
- **Build goal (draft):** 30 posts in 30 days on their own accounts.
- **In the box:**
  - a line bank in house style
  - card templates
  - a 30-day calendar builder
  - music rotation rules
  - captions for each platform
  - the weekly scorecard
- **Next kit if they miss:** K3 (YouTube) or K4 (all-in-one).
- **Proof story:** ADHD Entrepreneur is the live example, built in public. It shows the system, never income or follower promises.
- **Research needed:** what Zucc learns from our own analytics after the first weeks.
- **Status:** idea

### K3. YouTube channel kit (faceless)
- **For:** someone who wants a channel without being on camera.
- **Build goal (draft):** channel set up and 4 videos published on a fixed schedule within the deadline.
- **In the box:** a pipeline from research to script, title and thumbnail brief, plus an upload checklist and AI setups for each step.
- **Next kit if they miss:** K2 (daily content system).
- **Research needed:** current YouTube rules on AI-generated content and on reused or music content. Zucc's creator notes.
- **Status:** idea

### K4. All-in-one launch kit: socials, website and a first product
- **For:** someone starting from zero who wants the whole setup.
- **Build goal (draft):** accounts set up, a simple website live with a working checkout (tested with a test order), and the first 14 posts out.
- **In the box:** K2, plus a site template, a product page template and a checkout setup guide.
- **Next kit if they miss:** whichever piece stalled (K2, K5 or K6).
- **Research needed:** which site builder and checkout to standardize on, and their current prices.
- **Status:** idea. This is the bigger, higher-priced kit.

### K5. Online store / dropshipping kit
- **For:** someone who wants to test a product without guessing.
- **Follows** `dropshipping/PLAYBOOK.md`: honest math first, written stop rules before any spending, and no revenue claims anywhere.
- **Build goal (draft):** one product test launched with a written budget cap and written stop rules. The goal is not sales.
- **In the box:**
  - the product criteria checklist
  - the honest-math sheet
  - a guide to the public ad library
  - a funnel split-test setup
  - a stop-rules template
- **Next kit if they miss:** K6 (paid ads).
- **Research needed:** Wil's dropshipping notes, Zucc's research, and current platform fees, all rechecked at build time.
- **Status:** idea

### K6. Paid ads starter
- **For:** someone ready to spend a small, fixed test budget for the first time.
- **Build goal (draft):** the first campaign is live with a budget cap and written stop rules, and tracking is checked with a test conversion.
- **In the box:** a campaign structure template, a stop-rules template, a creative brief builder, and a tracking checklist.
- **Next kit if they miss:** K5, or a one-on-one review if Wil adds coaching later.
- **Research needed:** current ad platform policies and setup steps.
- **Status:** idea

### K7. AI for a local service business
- **For:** owners of trades and home-service businesses such as cleaning, landscaping and washing.
- **Build goal (draft):** the follow-up automation handles a test lead from start to finish (quote, follow-up, review request) and is switched on for real leads.
- **In the box:** quote and follow-up templates, a review-request flow, scheduling notes, and AI setups.
- **Next kit if they miss:** K1 (The Operator).
- **Note:** this draws on real experience running a local service business. It stays faceless and names no business.
- **Status:** idea
