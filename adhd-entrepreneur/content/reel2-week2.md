## The quote bar (Wil, Oct 28)

- One idea, said bluntly to "you".
- Concrete and zero filler.
- Useful in a Gen Z starter's actual day: starting, overthinking, building something next to a 9-to-5.
- No slang or gimmicks.

**The brand rules still hold:**
- Original wording. Never reuse or paraphrase anyone's known line, and never name the people the bar is modeled on.
- No income or medical claims.
- "Next to a 9-to-5" means building something of your own. Never promise it replaces a paycheck.
