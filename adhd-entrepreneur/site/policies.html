Refund policy and guarantee

Starter kits are digital products sold as a one-time purchase in US dollars. Before you pay, each kit's page shows its price, its build goal, the deadline and the proof we accept.

Finish the goal, get your money back

If you complete your kit's build goal by its deadline and send us the proof listed on the kit's page, we refund the full price to your original payment method. To claim it, email us from the address you bought with, within 14 days after the deadline, with your proof attached.

If you don't finish

You keep the kit, and we send you a code for 50% off the next kit that covers what stopped you. The code is good for 60 days.

If something doesn't work

If a kit's files or setups don't work as described and we can't fix the problem within 7 days of you telling us, we refund you in full.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the card or account you paid with. Your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to show them.

What the guarantee is not

The build goal is always something you set up or complete. It is never an amount of money earned. We don't promise income, sales or any other business result.

Terms of service

What you get

A starter kit is a set of digital files: setup instructions, prompts, templates and guides. You can use them in your own business. Please don't resell them or share the files publicly.

Tools from other companies

Kits work with AI tools and apps run by other companies, such as Claude. You sign up for those tools yourself, pay them directly, and their own terms apply. They can change their features or prices at any time. We're not responsible for them.

Referral links

Some links to those tools are referral links. If you sign up through one, we may get a credit or a commission. We only recommend a tool when it fits the kit.

No promised results

Kits and posts are educational. What you get out of them depends on your work and your situation. Nothing we publish is legal, financial, tax or medical advice.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Prices are in US dollars, and taxes may be added where they apply. The guarantee and refunds work as described in the refund policy above.

Changes

We may update these policies. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version. A purchase is covered by the version in place on the day you paid.

Privacy policy

What we collect

  • When you buy: your name, your email and the details needed to process the payment. Stripe handles your card details. We never see your full card number.
  • When you email us: your address and whatever you write.
  • When you visit this site: our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and reliability.

What we don't do

This site has no ad-platform tracking pixels and no analytics cookies. We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with ad platforms.

How we use it

To deliver your kit, run the guarantee and refunds, answer your emails and keep records the law requires.

Services we use

Stripe for payments, Google for email, and our website host. Each handles your data under its own privacy policy.

Your choices
