FAQ

Straight answers.

Is this a program for ADHD?

No. Nothing here is medical, and we don't diagnose or treat anything. The name is about a working style: lots of ideas, fast starts, and needing a clear finish line. The kits are for anyone who works that way.

Will a kit make me money?

We don't promise that. Every build goal is something you set up or finish, never an amount you earn. What your business makes depends on your work, your market and a fair bit of luck.

Which AI do I need?

The kits are written for Claude. If you use ChatGPT or Gemini, each kit will list what carries over before you buy. You sign up for those tools yourself and pay them directly.

How does the refund work?

Finish the build goal by the deadline, email us the proof listed on your kit, and we refund the full price to your original payment method. If you miss it, there's no refund, and the kit stays yours. Full terms.

When can I buy?

Not yet. The first kits are being built and tested now. Take the quiz to find your kit, and email us from the result page to hear when it launches.
