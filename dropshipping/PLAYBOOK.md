# Branded Dropshipping — Combined Build Plan

Source: two videos by Mark Builds Brands.

- **Video 1:** "i made $639,513.58 in 90 days with branded dropshipping (just copy me)" (27:14, posted Nov 6, 2024). The branded-funnel playbook. Dashboard: $639,513.58 over 90 days (Aug 7 to Nov 4, 2024), 526,371 sessions, $33.70 AOV.
- **Video 2:** "ZERO to $1k/day in 3 days with ai dropshipping" (25:11). The AI speed-run case study. Dashboard: $4,092.83 gross, 71 orders, Jul 16–20. Later: 1.54% CVR, $57.64 AOV.

**Audience:** Wil, a beginner. Runs a local window-washing business and has never run paid ads. Teach each step. Check every number. Never make up traffic, revenue or product data.

---

## Flags (always apply)

1. **Neither revenue claim is verified.** Both come from his own screenshots. Treat the videos as a method, not a promise.
2. **His real business is selling to viewers.** He pitches his "Brand Builders Academy" mentorship and his GetHookd.ai tool in every video. Do not recommend buying either one.
3. **Neither video gives a real testing budget.** Paid traffic is required. Phase 0 exists because "just copy me" hides the tests that failed.

## Phase 0 — The honest math

Work out the real startup cost **before** anything else:

- Ad testing budget **per product test**
- Funnelish or Checkout Champ subscription
- Shopify plan
- Domain
- Sample orders for filming creatives

Look up current prices when doing this. Do not use remembered prices.

Teach the losing scenario:

- A **$100 CPA against a $50 product is a dead product**, unless the funnel raises AOV. Video 1 claims post-purchase offers lift AOV from about $100 to $130–145 or more (see the AOV caveat above).
- Unit math per order = AOV after upsells − product cost − shipping − processing fees − CPA. If that is negative, scaling only multiplies the loss.

**Gate:** Wil approves, in writing, a budget he is willing to **lose completely** before Phase 1 starts.

---

## Phase 1 — Product strategy

- **Video 1:** don't hunt for secret products. Pick products **already doing $10M or more** (saturated and proven) and win on marketing, funnel and unit economics. Quote (6:24–6:41): "you'll see them and be like yeah I've seen that product before… that's the difference between me and 99% of people".
- **Video 2:** "winning products are CREATED through testing, not found."
- **Where they agree:** picking a product is a process, not a lucky find.

---

## Phase 2 — Product criteria (Video 2's checklist; apply to every candidate)

1. Solves a **painful** problem in a **passionate** market.
2. **Margin rules:** a minimum gross margin, plus a pricing rule set as a multiple of cost. Work out the exact multiple with Wil; the product must survive ad costs.
3. **Shoebox-sized:** cheap to ship, easy logistics.
4. **High LTV potential:** a natural upsell, a subscription angle, or both.

---

## Phase 3 — Find candidates (two ways)

- **Method A (Video 1): native ads.** Browse native ad grids on big publisher sites. His demo was weather.com's "sponsored content" section. He quotes native CPMs of $3–10 against Facebook's $20–30+ in Q4; these are 2024 figures, so re-check them.
- **Method B (Video 2): the Facebook Ad Library.** Scan it for winning ads. He demos this with his GetHookd tool's MCP connector inside Claude. **Do not buy GetHookd.** Do the same workflow by hand: hooks, angles and formats are all visible in the public Ad Library.

**Output:** 5 candidates, each with the product, its price, and the angle its ads use.

---

## Phase 4 — Validate with data

- **Video 1's bar:** 200,000–300,000+ monthly SimilarWeb visitors, which he equates to about $300k–500k/month in ad spend. A product that clears it counts as validated.
- Drop anything below the bar.
- Show Wil the real numbers. If a number can't be checked, say so. Never estimate traffic figures.

---

## Phase 5 — Funnel: run the split test (the videos disagree, so test it)

**Video 1's rule:** never send ads to a plain Shopify page. Build a 4-stage funnel in Funnelish or Checkout Champ, with orders landing in Shopify:

1. **The ad sells the problem or idea, not the product.** Target 7–10% CTR, against about 3% for a standard ad.
2. **Advertorial pre-sale page.** It builds authority and frames the problem, but does **not** sell.
3. **VSL page** (video sales letter) with no distractions.
4. **Order page.** His example price is $97.

**Video 2's counter:** he sent ads **directly to the product page** with no pre-lander, as a day-one split test against the funnel version. He cut the loser after about $300–400 of spend and still hit $1k/day in 3 days.

**What to do:** build **both** from day one (a simple theme is fine) and split the traffic. Drop the loser at the $300–400 spend mark. Wil's own numbers settle the debate, not either video's rule.

---

## Phase 6 — Post-purchase profit (build it BEFORE launch)

**Video 1's profit layer:**

- One-click VSL upsell right after checkout ($200–300 offers)
- 50%-off downsell if they decline
- Order bumps
- Email and SMS in roughly the first 24 hours after purchase
- Target AOV lift: about $100 → $130–145 or more

**Video 2's warning:** he launched **without** upsells, and his AOV stalled at $57.64, below his target. That failure is the proof. Don't repeat it.

**Table Wil must see before scaling:**

| CPA | Front-end price | AOV after upsells | Profit per order |
|---|---|---|---|

If the last column isn't positive (green), don't scale.

---

## Phase 7 — Ad account structure (Video 2's exact setup)

- One campaign per landing page.
- 4 ad sets per campaign, each using a **different** creative format.
- 3–5 ads per ad set. Broad targeting.
- Meta's automated enhancements: **OFF**.
- **Write the stop rules BEFORE spending.** For example: drop an angle after $X spent with no add-to-cart.

---

## Phase 8 — Creatives: an all-AI workflow (Video 2)

**His tools:** AI animations, static images styled like native ads, podcast-style creatives, and image-to-video generation with repeated revisions.

**Wil's version:** a phone-and-laptop workflow that copies the **formats**, since the tool itself can't be used without buying it:

- 3 angle variants per product
- UGC-style hooks that open with the problem
- One new batch of creatives per test round

Creatives are half the business. His closing advice: spend your time on the store/funnel and the creatives, nothing else.

---

## Phase 9 — What goes wrong (from Video 2's own log; plan for all of it)

- **Bot traffic inflates sessions.** His run showed 4,471 sessions at 1.54% CVR. Judge results by purchases and MER (total revenue ÷ total ad spend), not session counts.
- **Ad accounts get shut down mid-run.** His was. Set up **backup ad accounts** before putting a single dollar into scaling.
- **AOV stalls without upsells.** Phase 6 is required, not optional.

3. Whether he wants to pick from the 5 candidates himself, or have Claude recommend the niche.
