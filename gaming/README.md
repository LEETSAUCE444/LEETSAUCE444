# Handheld gaming setup

Setup notes for Wil's three emulation devices: Miyoo Mini Plus (Onion OS), Retroid Pocket 3/3+ and a Galaxy S24-series phone.

- `handheld-hub.html`: the guide page (setup checklists, what runs where, Wii-on-Retroid settings, official emulator links, legal ways to get games). Published at https://claude.ai/artifact/FfwbzUamu8kShBwFyTYq1d
- `research/`: fact-checked research notes behind the page, with sources. Checked 2026-10-29; items marked UNVERIFIED could not be confirmed from a primary source.

No ROMs, ISOs or BIOS files are stored or linked here. Games come from dumping carts and discs Wil owns, or from free homebrew.
